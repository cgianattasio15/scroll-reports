What We Built Together

The Arc

A plain-English summary of the 8-month engagement.

The engagement was structured around a focused content strategy: prioritizing the formats and signals that drive the right audience to action. Over the 8 months, the goal was to position you as the perimenopause skin authority and build a content engine that converts the right audience into Skin Quiz completions and product buyers. October was the strategy build (voice, audience, content pillars, conversion architecture) with posting beginning in November. The early months built the foundation. From January onwards, CTR and PCR consistently exceeded the Rise stage target range, evidence that visitors who landed on the profile were ready to act. The bigger strategic question was depth: were people saving the content, watching it through? In Q2, we made an explicit commitment to grow Saves and Retention by pivoting the content format toward educational MOFU Reels that named the perimenopause experience specifically. April was the diagnostic month. May was the proof. Saves jumped 80% MoM, Retention recovered 10 points, and the May 25 Dark Spots Reel drove 62 saves on its own, the engagement Saves record. The strategy worked. The content engine is yours to keep running.

A Note on Retention

Reading the Data Honestly

Retention % declined January to April before recovering in May. The honest read is more nuanced. Watch time in absolute seconds actually improved (January 8.4s to March 11.9s) as Reels got longer. The percentage dropped because the denominator grew, not because the content got less engaging. When the Q2 commitment surfaced retention as a focus, we recalibrated content length and approach. May closed with retention at 31% (+10pp MoM) and the May 18 Tough Love Reel hit 77% retention, the engagement record. The lesson: depth signals are about more than one ratio.

The Patterns

What Worked Architecturally

Four lessons the engagement proved about Sylvia’s audience and the business.

Audience

The IVP Pattern

Women 40 to 60 experiencing hormonal skin changes responded most when content named the experience specifically. “Perimenopause,” “after 40,” “hormonal shifts” functioned as recognition signals, not just keywords.

Format

The Educational MOFU Reel

Reels that named a specific outcome and delivered a clear sequence (“dark spots plus the right plan,” “5 things you’re doing wrong”) consistently outperformed across the engagement. This is the format template.

Conversion

The Conversion Architecture

The Skin Quiz worked as the primary CTA from Month 3 onwards. CTR and PCR both exceeded the Rise stage range throughout. The infrastructure is built and proven.

Hooks

Hook Patterns That Move Metrics

Three structures consistently outperformed: the “are you doing this” callout for community engagement (May 1 SPF Reel, 60 comments), the contrarian “I would never but” setup for retention (May 18 Tough Love Reel, 77% retention), and the “specific outcome plus the right plan” promise for saves (May 25 Dark Spots Reel, 62 saves). Match the hook to the metric you most want to move.
