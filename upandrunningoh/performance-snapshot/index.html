The Big Picture

Where things stand

Since October, the goal has been simple. Get Up and Running in front of the right local audience, build a real community around the brand, and turn that attention into store visits, fittings, and loyal customers. The first half of 2026 built a strong foundation. Your monthly performance score held in a healthy band, between 7.9 and 8.3, every single month.

The Honest Picture

Where we’re struggling

Three areas holding performance back. Open each one for the detail. These are the parts of the strategy we are most focused on fixing next.

Reach

Reach is inconsistent

Big months, then quiet months. The swing is the problem. +

Some months reach a lot of new people, others pull back. Total views swung from around 45,000 in April to about 16,000 in May. When we post the community content that travels, reach jumps. The fix is a steadier drumbeat of it, so reach compounds instead of spiking and dropping.

45k April views16k May views
Engagement

The conversation is quiet

People are watching, but few are talking back. +

Comments and replies are the softest area. Comments dropped to single digits in May. That two-way engagement is what builds loyalty, and it is also a signal that tells the algorithm to show your content to more people. Quiet comments hold back both relationship and reach.

3 comments in May15+ the target
Stories · biggest opportunity

Stories are underperforming

A lot of effort going in, not much coming back out. +

This is the biggest opportunity on the account. You are posting a lot of stories, but each one reaches only a small slice of your followers, and they generate very few replies, often fewer than five a month across dozens of stories. The good news: a more focused, interactive story strategy can turn this around quickly. You flagged stories yourself, and the data agrees with you.

~90 stories / month<5 replies / month

The Plan

The plan in action

Four focused moves for the next phase. Open each for exactly what it looks like in your feed and stories. No change to your current scope, just a sharper strategy aimed at the gaps above. Each move ties back to one or more of your goals.

Move 01

Build a consistent community drumbeat

Make the content that travels a weekly habit, not a one-off. +

Lock a steady weekly cadence of the community and event content that already travels for you, so reach builds month over month instead of spiking once and fading.

  • A recurring “Faces of the Run Club” customer and runner spotlight.
  • Event recaps from 5Ks and group runs, on a set rhythm.
  • Team features that put the people behind the store front and center.
Serves: Community-forward brand
Move 02

Start more conversations

The fastest lever for comments, replies, and reach. +

Put one clear question on every post, and add interactive stickers to stories. The fastest way to rebuild comments and replies, and the same lever that pushes reach back up.

  • Story polls like “road or trail?” and “this or that” gear matchups.
  • Caption prompts like “what is your next race?”
  • Quick question stickers that invite a tap, not a paragraph.
Serves: Community + engagement
Move 03

Give people a reason to save and come back

Reference content runners bookmark, plus the app. +

Build save-worthy reference content runners bookmark, and use it to put the Up and Running app in front of them as a tool they return to, not just download.

  • “Which shoe for your foot type” guides and fit-tip carousels.
  • “What to wear on race day” checklists.
  • Simple, real walkthroughs of the Up and Running app.
Serves: Local shopping + app promotion
Move 04 · growth

Put corporate fittings on the map, and fix stories

Document the service, win the next business. +

Fewer, sharper, more authentic stories built to invite interaction, plus a dedicated series documenting your on-site corporate fittings, which doubles as proof that wins the next business.

  • An “On the Road” series from corporate fit days.
  • A clear “book a fit day for your team” path.
  • Raw behind-the-scenes and shop-local moments in stories.
Serves: Corporate fittings + community + local shopping
